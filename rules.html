Server rules and terms

Last updated 9 November 2026

Ashen is free to play. By playing you agree to these rules. Raiding, fighting and betraying other survivors is part of the game. The rules are about what happens outside the game's own rules.

Play fair

  • No cheats, hacks, modified game pages, bots or scripts that play for you.
  • No using bugs to duplicate items, get into places you shouldn't, or crash the server. If you find one, tell the admin. That's appreciated.
  • Don't attack, overload or try to break into the server, the website or anyone's account.
  • One character per person to get around a ban, kick or limit is not allowed.

Chat and names

  • No hate, slurs, threats, harassment or sexual content. Trash talk about the game is fine; going after someone personally is not.
  • Don't share anyone's personal information (real name, address, school, photos, accounts), including your own.
  • No spam, scams or advertising.
  • Names must follow the same rules. Don't pretend to be the admin or another player.

What happens if you break them

The admin can kick you, delete things you built, or ban you. A ban covers your character and your internet address. The admin decides, and doesn't have to warn you first. If you think a ban was a mistake, get in touch: (only for data deletion requests and ban appeals; include your in-game name).

Terms of use

  • No guarantees. Ashen is provided as it is. The server runs on a home computer and can be offline, reset or shut down at any time. Characters, items and buildings can be lost, and the game can change.
  • Age. Ashen is not made for children under 13. If you are under 13, ask a parent or guardian first.
  • Your data. What the server records and why is in the privacy notice.
  • The game. The game, its art and its code belong to its maker. You may stream it and post videos and screenshots of it, but you may not copy, modify, rehost or redistribute the game or its code.
