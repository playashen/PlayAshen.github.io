About Ashen

Ashen (PlayAshen) is a free multiplayer zombie survival game you play in your browser, kind of like an .io game. You don't need to download anything or make an account. You chop trees, mine rocks, craft tools, build a base, and try not to die when the zombies come out at night. It works in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari and Opera on a computer.

  • Zombies come every night, and new kinds show up the longer you survive.
  • There are workbenches, a smelter, guns, bows and explosives to work up to.
  • Teams can have up to 7 people. You can share a base or raid other players.
  • The map has abandoned houses, a city full of lava and a desert with snakes.
