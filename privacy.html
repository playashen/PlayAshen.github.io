Privacy notice

Last updated 9 November 2026

In short: to run the game, stop cheaters and see how to make it better, the Ashen server records your in-game name, your internet address, your rough location (country), and how you play. It is kept on the computer that runs the server, never sold, never used for ads, and deleted after 180 days. You can ask for it to be deleted at any time.

Who runs Ashen

Ashen is a small independent game. Its server runs on a home computer owned by the person who made it ("we" in this notice). Contact: (only for data deletion requests and ban appeals; include your in-game name).

We do not use cookies, advertising, or third-party analytics or tracking. We do not ask for your real name, email or age, so please don't use your real name as your in-game name.

What your browser keeps

Your browser stores a random key so the server knows it's you next time, plus your game settings. This uses the browser's local storage, only for the game to work. Clearing your browser data removes it, and you start again as a new player.

Who else is involved

  • Cloudflare carries the connection between your browser and the game server, so it sees your internet address. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • GitHub hosts this website (the Ashen page, guide and this notice), so it sees your internet address when you open them. See GitHub's privacy statement.

We never sell or share what we record with anyone else, except if the law requires it.

Why we are allowed to

We record this because we have a legitimate interest in running the game, keeping it fair and safe, and improving it. We keep it to what's needed and delete it on a timer.

Children

Ashen is not made for children under 13. If you are under 13, ask a parent or guardian before playing. If you believe a child under 13 has played, contact us and we will delete their data.

Your rights

You can ask to see what we have recorded about you, to correct it, or to delete it. Contact us with your in-game name (and roughly when you played), and we will do it within 30 days. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may also have the right to complain to your data protection authority.

Changes

If we change what we record, we will update this page and the date at the top.
