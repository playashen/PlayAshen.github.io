How to play Ashen

Ashen is a free multiplayer zombie survival game in your browser. You spawn with nothing and zombies come out at night. Here's what you need to know for your first few nights.

Your first day

A day lasts 8 minutes and a night lasts 8 minutes. The clock at the top of the screen counts down to nightfall.

  1. Punch trees and rocks for wood and stone. Berry bushes and orange trees give food.
  2. Craft a hatchet and a pickaxe (press C). They gather a lot faster, and the pickaxe gives the most XP per swing.
  3. Check the houses. Boxes, trash cans and chests refill over time.
  4. Get some walls, a door and a campfire up before it gets dark.

Zombies ignore you for the first 12 minutes after you spawn, so your first night is pretty easy. If you hit one, that kind of zombie will come after you every night from then on.

Surviving the night

  • Your heat drains at night. Stay near a campfire, carry a torch, or wear a Leather Jacket or Arctic Suit. Once your heat runs out you start losing health.
  • Zombies go for you when you're on their screen, and they attack buildings. Walls slow them down, but you'll still need a weapon.
  • In daylight zombies are slow and weak, and they burn up during the morning.

Zombie types

A zombie with a red outline is coming for you. Tougher kinds start attacking the longer you have played.

Building a base

Wood walls and doors come first; stone walls and doors are far tougher. Only you and your team can open your doors and chests. Put a campfire inside for warmth and cooking. Food rots in your bag but stays fresh in a chest.

Benches unlock better gear: the workbench, the research bench, the smelter (for alloy, using gas from barrels), and more. A hammer and dynamite are how you break into other players' bases.

Crafting, levels and skills

Gathering, crafting and killing zombies give XP. Each level gives a skill point, which you can spend on recipes or skills like Backpack (+2 inventory slots per rank) and Master Builder. Higher levels unlock guns, armour, explosives and tools that gather iron and sulfur.

Food and cooking

  • Berries and oranges can be eaten straight away. Hold left click with food in your hand.
  • Raw meat from deer and boar must be cooked at a campfire first.
  • Food rots in your bag. The small bar on each food icon shows how fresh it is: green, then yellow, then red.

Teams and PvP

Ashen is multiplayer: everyone on the server shares one world. Make a team of up to 7 to share a base, doors and chests. Other survivors can attack you, but you are protected for a short while after you join or respawn, until you attack someone. When a player dies, what they carried drops where they fell.

Dying and respawn kits

You keep your level, skills and unlocked recipes when you die. From level 4 you respawn with a kit that grows with your level: tools, wood and stone at first, then walls, doors, benches, and later guns and armour. Killing other players adds a bonus on top, from a bigger backpack to a drill and dynamite. A kit is ready again 10 minutes after the last one.

Questions

Is Ashen free?

Yes. No download, no account, no payments.

Which browsers does it work in?

Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari and Opera on a computer, with a keyboard and mouse.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, on the server. Your browser keeps the key to your character, so play from the same browser to carry on where you left off.

Why is the server sometimes offline?

Ashen is a small indie game and its server runs on a home PC. When it is switched off, the front page says so and checks again by itself.
